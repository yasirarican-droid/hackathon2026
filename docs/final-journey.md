# Three quiet explorers: completion and farewell

The current scope remains Apollo 11, Viking 1 and Sojourner. The application is English. Readable Nunito headings and Patrick Hand body text are separated from Share Tech Mono on live CRT contents and close-up CRT stories. Raster illustrations are unchanged.

## NSSDCA visibly used

Each Where is it now? card begins with a direct NASA NSSDCA Master Catalog link. Verified 15 November 2026 local time, using the original NASA XHTML records retrieved directly because the web reader rejected their text/xml content type:

- Apollo 11 LM / EASEP: https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=1969-059C — descent stage as launch platform, left on the Moon; ascent stage fate explicitly unknown and impact only assumed.
- Viking 1 lander: https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=1975-075C — stationary lander, Chryse Planitia and operation through November 1982.
- Sojourner: https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=MESURPR — all rover communication through the lander, science purpose and last communication. MESURPR is the actual catalog identifier linked by the Pathfinder record, not 1996-068B.
- Pathfinder: https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=1996-068A — stationary lander, Ares Vallis and 27 September 1997 communication loss.

Catalog history does not establish present exact rover coordinates. Dated LRO/HiRISE images and later NASA/JPL discussion have their own citations. The cards do not pretend NSSDCA supports every later interpretation. Viking catalog and NASA Science differ on the November day; user-facing farewell retains month precision.

## Last Signal

Each console has a Last Signal button. Four short paragraphs use a modern narrator, not invented robot dialogue or a claimed archival last transmission. Sources remain available below the CRT. The device is thanked for its contribution; location and present inactivity are explained, with upper-stage and final-rover-position uncertainty preserved.

Normal motion types each paragraph at 22 ms per character, then allows 5.5 seconds before continuing. Pause and Next are available. Reduced motion shows complete paragraphs and requires Next, avoiding forced timing. Full text is also available as a readable transcript. Completing the final paragraph runs a gentle 1.9-second static-to-black transition and returns home. Skip returns home without marking the story complete. Close/Escape returns to the console and stops the sequence. Hidden pages suspend its clock and sound. The optional, low-volume 1.5-second procedural static is an interface effect, not original mission audio. No new narration recording has been generated or substituted. External recording scripts are in the owner's Downloads/Last Signal Seslendirme Metinleri directory.

## Explorer certificate and reset

The home certificate button is always available. Its checklist shows the remaining requirements. Unlock requires all 34 listed mission objects (11 Apollo, 10 Viking, 13 Sojourner), all nine Field Guide answers, all nine notebook discovery answers, all three status cards and all three completed Last Signal stories. Cinematic playback is optional and does not prevent accessible direct exploration.

The earned certificate is a downloadable, printable SVG with an optional name and local download date. The name is neither persisted nor uploaded. It is an independent project acknowledgment, not a NASA-issued certificate or evidence of measured learning. Existing per-mission badges remain separate. Visits, answers and story completion persist locally when storage is available; they remain usable in memory if blocked.

Reset missions on home asks once before clearing all mission visits, goals, quizzes, naming-story read state, farewell/status completion and entry preferences on this device. Cancellation preserves progress. It does not clear unrelated browser data or download caches.
