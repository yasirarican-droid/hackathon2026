# Guided mission quizzes and discovery awards

A mission certificate is earned by correctly answering that mission's three notebook-quiz questions. The third correct answer immediately displays the certificate and a short 48-piece confetti burst; no additional Next/Finish click is needed. All three mission quizzes earn the combined certificate. Visits, optional Field Guide practice, status cards and Last Signal completion are no longer certificate locks.

Each quiz shows three numbered steps, one clear question, an actual exhibit clue, supportive wrong-answer feedback, a return-to-quiz control on the clue, and a correct-answer explanation. The next correct-answer state is saved immediately. Completed awards have a separate completedMissions record, so practicing a quiz again cannot remove an earned certificate. Existing saved full quiz completions are migrated when progress is restored. Reset missions clears both current quiz answers and earned awards after confirmation.

The certificate page leads directly into the selected mission's actual quiz rather than leaving the visitor at an unrelated room or object. Each earned mission certificate can be reopened from the central certificate page. The main home button identifies the combined certificate when all three quizzes are passed. The Field Guide is optional practice and now has a clear entry into the reward quiz.

Objects now lists only unique mission panels attached to a visible console/desk control. Apollo has 11, Viking 10 and Sojourner 11. A separate radio transcript, floating home artifacts and Sojourner's solar/relay/timeline detail pages are not standalone room tasks. Those supporting pages can remain inside relevant evidence flows. The Viking weather paper opens the weather record it displays. The Sojourner communications practice points to the actual rover/lander explorer. A visited check means opened, not learned; quiz progress and earned awards are tracked separately.

Certificate design includes a decorative double border, stars, mission band and project seal. The optional name and three color themes update an SVG preview immediately. Downloads preserve the chosen name and theme. The name is neither saved in localStorage nor sent to a server. Downloaded SVG can be opened and printed. No NASA logo, endorsement or NASA-issued qualification is claimed. The award recognizes completion of a guided quiz, not independently measured learning effectiveness. Reduced motion and Pause motion suppress falling confetti while keeping the completion banner.

Browser acceptance: actual-room menu membership, visited ticks, certificate-to-quiz and clue-return navigation, wrong-answer guidance, immediate completion, confetti, per-mission/combined certificate, live name/theme customization, escaped SVG download, privacy, retained award after practice/reload, landscape controls and confirmed full reset. New functional checks are in scripts/quiz-reward-check.mjs. The full journey runner combines those checks with the recorded Last Signal suite.
