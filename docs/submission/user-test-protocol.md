# Five-person exploratory usability check

This is a protocol, not a completed study. No user-test results are claimed.

Use anonymous labels A–E. Do not record names, contact details, voices or images of children. A supervising adult can guide the observation. Explain that the application, not the participant, is being tested.

Allow about five minutes per person. Use a real target device in landscape. Start with a clean browser state or reset the saved practice progress.

1. Ask the participant to enter a mission without watching its story. Observe whether the direct-entry option is understood.
2. Ask them to find one original NASA photograph and its source. Observe whether illustration and evidence are distinguished.
3. Ask them to open the Field guide, follow one clue and explain their answer in their own words.
4. In Sojourner, ask them to find the part that helps provide power using the 3D view or labeled buttons.
5. Refresh the page, return to the Field guide and observe whether saved progress is understandable.

Before and after the activity, ask one mission-specific question without prompting the answer: Apollo—what stayed on the Moon? Viking—does one intriguing signal prove life? Sojourner—how did the lander and rover work together?
