# The student behind Sojourner's name

Verified on 14 November 2026 against NASA/JPL's original 14 July 1995 announcement, NASA Names First Rover to Explore the Surface of Mars:
https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/nasa-names-first-rover-to-explore-the-surface-of-mars/

The original announcement identifies a worldwide student essay contest, the winning entry by 12-year-old Valerie Ambroise from Bridgeport, Connecticut, the Sojourner Truth namesake, her antislavery/women's-rights work and the traveler meaning of Sojourner. The relevant paragraphs begin with The name Sojourner was chosen and Valerie Ambroise. The announcement says NASA selected the name following the competition; the app distinguishes the student's winning proposal from the agency's formal selection.

https://www.nasa.gov/podcasts/on-a-mission/first-steps-sojourner-s4e2/
