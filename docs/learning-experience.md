# Evidence-led exploration

The homepage opens each archive directly. Watch the story separately restarts its journey, supplied film and synchronized CRT introduction.

The Field guide contains three sourced goals per mission. Opening an exhibit counts as a visit; answering its evidence question completes a goal. Incorrect choices provide a hint. Completion produces a recap and downloadable text field notes with NASA links. Progress stays in the visitor's browser, can be reset, and falls back to the current visit when local storage is unavailable.

Apollo and Viking retain their NASA model geometry and offer camera-guided functional labels, without claiming semantic mesh identification. Model camera guidance is an educational view, not an engineering measurement.
