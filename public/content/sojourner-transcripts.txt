A team across space
Modern interview with Dave Gruel
Source: 31:59.50 - 32:29.85

Dave Gruel · modern interview: The communications link between the lander and the rover was pretty basic.

Dave Gruel · modern interview: You know, we think about the fact that we can communicate with a vehicle that’s on the surface of Mars, that’s millions of miles away, and the power that it gives off is similar to the power of a light bulb in your refrigerator.

Dave Gruel · modern interview: And we can make out those ones and zeros here on Earth and figure out what that says about the surface of another planet, yet we couldn’t get these two vehicles, who are in close proximity to one another, to communicate well.
